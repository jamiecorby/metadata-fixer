Metadata Fixer

Privacy

Last updated 20 October 2026

Metadata Fixer is built so that your photos and videos stay on your device. This page explains exactly what leaves it, and why.

Your files

Reading and writing metadata happens inside your browser, using ExifTool compiled to WebAssembly. Your photos and videos are not uploaded to us or anyone else. The fixed copies are created in your browser and saved by you.

AI suggestions

Only when you ask for an AI suggestion, a reduced copy of that one image (at most 1024 pixels, re-encoded so that it carries no metadata) is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and passed to an AI model (Anthropic's Claude, or Google's Gemini if we have enabled it) to estimate the date and place. With it we send the file name and any trip details you entered. We do not store the image or the answer. The provider processes it under its commercial or paid API terms, which do not allow it to train models on your content. We keep a usage record (time, success, token counts, model) to run the service and prevent abuse.

If you choose to use your own Anthropic API key instead, the image goes directly from your browser to Anthropic under your own agreement with them, and never passes through us.

Authenticity check

The check itself runs in your browser on the file's metadata and Content Credentials. Only if you press the optional visual check is a reduced, metadata-free copy of that one image (at most 1024 pixels) sent to our server and passed to the same AI model; unlike AI suggestions, no file name or trip details are sent. The image and the answer are not stored. We keep the same usage record as for AI suggestions. The result is advisory and not proof.

Accounts and payments

If you create an account we store your email address, your credit balance and a ledger of credit changes. Sign-in uses one-time email links. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store card details, and receive only confirmation of what was bought.

Maps and place search

The map uses OpenStreetMap tiles, and place search uses the Photon service (komoot), so map views and the places you search for are sent to those services. They are only contacted when you open the map or search.

Stored in your browser

Your settings, templates and last used settings are kept in your browser's local storage. If you are signed in, templates and last used settings are also saved to your account so they are available on your other devices. A session token keeps you signed in. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Service providers

  • Supabase (database, sign-in and server functions)
  • Anthropic and Google (AI suggestions and the visual authenticity check)
  • Stripe (payments)
  • GitHub Pages (hosting of this website)
  • OpenStreetMap and komoot Photon (maps and place search)
  • Google Fonts (typefaces)

Your choices

You can use every fixing feature without an account. You can ask us to export or delete your account data at any time through the support link below; deleting your account removes your email, balance, ledger, usage records and saved templates.
