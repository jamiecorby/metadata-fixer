# Metadata Fixer - UI/UX pass, 8 Nov 2026

Branch `ux-pass`. Harness: `tests/ux.py` (part of `npm test`). Screenshots at 1440x900, 390x844 and 320x640, light and dark, are produced with `SHOTS=<dir> python3 tests/ux.py`.

## Scope and method

Web app (static, in-browser). Roles: one (the person fixing a library); surfaces: desktop browser, iPhone Safari, no print or email. Jobs walked: add files, review one file, change trip details, open Settings and Help, Sanitise screen. Binding rules: none changed (no pricing, credit, auth or metadata-writing logic touched).

Brand source: no `lookbook_metadata-fixer` row exists in D1 `ventures_marketing` (look books exist for Virra, GapScore, LegaCasa, Scorchly, Kitty). The existing tokens in `src/styles.css` (teal accent, IBM Plex Sans/Mono, slate neutrals tinted toward the accent, light and dark) were treated as the design system. OQ-1: create a look book row for Metadata Fixer from the current tokens? Default: no, leave as is.

Direction phase: not run. Look scored 4, above the trigger of 3, and no redesign was asked for. Mobbin and Screenrove were not used: nothing in this pass imitates another product, so there are no precedents to cite.

## Second pass, 9 Nov 2026

### Performance (Lighthouse 12.6.1, `npm run lighthouse`)

| Page | Live main before (mobile / desktop perf, SEO) | Branch after (all four categories) |
|-|-|-|
| index | 90 / 99, SEO 100 | 100 everywhere |
| privacy | 93 / 100, SEO 91 | 100 everywhere |
| terms | 99 / 100, SEO 91 | 100 everywhere |
| 404 | 93 / 100, SEO 91 | 100 everywhere |

Index mobile went from FCP 2.9 s / LCP 2.9 s to FCP 1.2 s / LCP 1.5 s (simulated Slow 4G). Changes:

- IBM Plex is self-hosted from `@fontsource` (SIL OFL 1.1, licence copied to `assets/fonts/`), so the third-party Google Fonts chain is gone. Two weights are preloaded. The privacy page no longer lists Google Fonts as a provider.
- The stylesheet is inlined at build time, which saves one round trip before first paint.
- The pricing cards are rendered into the HTML at build time. Pricing is now visible without script and causes no layout shift.
- `boot.js` starts the app after the page has painted and its fonts have arrived. Files chosen or dropped before that point are kept and handed to the app; a dropped file is never opened by the browser. This is tested.
- Privacy, terms and 404 now have meta descriptions.
- GitHub Pages fixes the cache lifetime at 10 minutes. Lighthouse reports this, but it does not affect the score.

### Content, data and edge cases

- Separators are now middle dots instead of " . ".
- File sizes use KB, MB and GB everywhere; small files no longer show "0.0 MB".
- Write summaries show dates as 2019-07-04 14:30:00, not the EXIF storage form 2019:07:04.
- The authenticity summary now adds up: checked = flagged + not enough evidence + look fine.
- One label per action: "Choose a folder" and "Choose on map" everywhere.
- 28 inline styles were replaced by spacing utilities on a `--s` scale. The colours on photo overlays and the dialog backdrop are now tokens. Motion uses duration and easing tokens.
- New tests cover long names, accented, emoji and CJK names at 320px; double presses on Mark ready and Export; and early input before the app starts.

### Feel

- Irreversible actions need a second press within 4 s, with a toast saying what will happen: Discard saved work, Reset a file, Clear sanitised copies and Delete template. Basis: Nielsen, error prevention.
- Export ends on a finish screen: "N files fixed and verified", what was checked and what to do next. Basis: Peak-End.
- The bottom bar has one primary action at a time. Once everything is exported, Save exported is the primary action. Basis: Hick.
- Thumbnails show a skeleton while they are being made. Under reduced motion it is static.
- On phones, dialogs are bottom sheets within thumb reach, and the primary action is full width at the bottom. Basis: Fitts. Evidence tier: Supported, Apple HIG sheets (recalled, unverified link).
- On Android, a short vibration marks export success and a double pulse marks failure. There is none on ordinary taps, and none under reduced motion.
- The landing page ends with a closing call to action. Evidence tier: Supported. Screenrove `check_my_ui` showed the Cal.com and Rallly homepages close on one, and that both carry customer proof. OQ-3: add customer proof? Default: no. There are no real testimonials yet, and none will be invented.
